About Malyuy!

Malyuy! is a simple, joyful drawing app made for kids and their creative minds — big colorful brushes, no clutter, made for little hands. We're a small team and we read every message that comes in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save or share my drawing?

Tap the save icon in the top toolbar to save your artwork to your device's Photos, or use the share icon to send it directly to family and friends.

My drawing disappeared — can I get it back?

Malyuy! keeps your most recent drawings in the Gallery inside the app. If something looks missing, check the Gallery first. If it's still not there, email us below and we'll help you look into it.

How do I restore a purchase I already made?

Tap the lock icon in the top-right corner of the home screen, then choose "Restore Purchases." This uses your Apple ID to restore anything you've already bought, at no extra cost.

Is Malyuy! safe for kids? Does it collect personal data?

Malyuy! is designed to be kid-safe: no ads targeting children, no chat, no external links inside the drawing experience. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data or account?

Email us at the address below with your request and we'll take care of it promptly.
