About Malyuy!

Malyuy! is a simple, joyful drawing app made for kids and their creative minds — big colorful brushes, no clutter, made for little hands. We're a small team and we read every message that comes in.

To see everything the app does, in 16 languages, visit malyuy.app.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save or share a drawing?

There's nothing to save while painting: every picture keeps your child's drawing by itself, and it's still there next time. To keep a copy in Photos, tap the download button (an arrow pointing into a tray) at the top of the drawing screen. The first time, a grown-up answers a quick math question and allows Malyuy! to add photos; after that it's one tap. From Photos you can share it like any other picture.

My drawing disappeared — can I get it back?

Each picture keeps its own drawing: open the theme it's in, and the drawing is on that picture's card. If it was wiped with Clear (the bin at the top of the drawing screen), tap Step back (the curved arrow) before leaving the page and it comes back. Drawings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them too; they're included in your device's iCloud or computer backup. Still missing? Email us below and we'll help you look into it.

What is free, and what does a purchase open?

The Christmas theme is free in full, every picture of it, and so is the first set of each game. Everything else, every other theme, the blank paper and the rest of the game sets, opens with one of two purchases in the shop: Everything, weekly, a plan that renews every week until you cancel it, or Everything, forever, one payment that never renews. Both open the same pictures, including every picture we add later. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency.

How does the weekly plan work, and how do I cancel it?

The plan is an App Store subscription: it renews every week and is charged to your Apple Account until you cancel it, and there is no free trial. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Malyuy!, or tap "Manage subscription" in the app's shop (behind the grown-up math question). Cancelling keeps everything open until the end of the week you have paid for. Apple handles the billing; we never see your card.

What happens to my child's drawings when the plan ends?

Nothing is ever deleted. When a plan ends, the pictures that came with it close again behind a padlock, with your child's paintings still on them, and they open the moment you subscribe again or buy "Everything, forever". The Christmas theme stays open whatever happens.

I bought a theme pack or "Everything else" before. What happens now?

You keep it. Single-theme packs and the "Everything else" upgrade are no longer sold, but everything you bought stays open on every device signed in to your Apple Account, and "Restore Purchases" brings it back after a reinstall. If you want the rest of the pictures, the shop offers the weekly plan or the one-time "Everything, forever"; the shop has one price for everyone, and a pack you already own stays yours alongside it.

How do I restore a purchase I already made?

Tap the padlock in the top-right corner of the screen where you choose a theme, answer the grown-up math question, then tap "Restore Purchases" in the shop. Make sure this device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring never costs anything, and it brings back the weekly plan, "Everything, forever" and any older theme pack alike.

How do I get a refund?

Every purchase and every renewal is made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account that paid, choose the charge and tell Apple what happened. Apple decides and answers within a day or two. If you need help finding the charge, email us and we will point you the right way; we cannot issue a refund ourselves.

Can my family share the purchase?

Yes. Both the weekly plan and "Everything, forever" support Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple Family group gets the same pictures on their own device. Turn on Family Sharing in Settings on your device, and on each family member's device tap "Restore Purchases" in the shop once.

Is Malyuy! safe for kids? Does it collect personal data?

Yes. Malyuy! has no ads of any kind, no chat, no accounts and no analytics or tracking, and it collects no data at all: drawings stay on your device. Everything meant for a grown-up, like the shop, rating the app, or saving to Photos for the first time, waits behind a grown-up math question. For full details, see our Privacy Policy and Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement, which are the app's terms of use.

How do I delete my data or account?

There's no account, and nothing is stored anywhere but on your device. To start one picture over, tap Clear (the bin) at the top of the drawing screen. To remove every drawing, delete the app. Your purchases stay with your Apple Account and come back with "Restore Purchases". Any other question about your child's data? Email us below.
